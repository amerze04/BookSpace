# BookSpace Visual Identity

BookSpace uses a **Premium Burgundy** visual identity: professional, refined, modern, and slightly warm without feeling overly corporate.

## Brand Colors

| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Deep Burgundy | `#6B1835` |
| Primary Hover | Dark Burgundy | `#57112A` |
| Primary Soft | Light Burgundy Tint | `#F4E9ED` |
| Secondary Accent | Muted Rose | `#B07A8D` |

## Neutral Colors

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| App Background | `#F7F4F3` |
| Surface / Cards | `#FFFFFF` |
| Primary Text | `#1D171A` |
| Secondary Text | `#6A6065` |
| Borders / Dividers | `#E5D8DD` |

## Semantic Colors

These should remain visually distinct from the burgundy brand color.

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Success | `#2E8B57` |
| Warning | `#C98900` |
| Error / Destructive | `#D63C3C` |

## General Style

- Use **burgundy selectively** for primary actions, active states, links, focus states, and branded areas.
- Do not make the whole interface burgundy; most application surfaces should remain neutral.
- Prefer white surfaces over the warm off-white app background.
- Use subtle borders and soft shadows rather than heavy elevation.
- Recommended corner radius: approximately `10–12px`.
- Keep layouts spacious and clean.
- The overall feel should be **professional, refined, slightly premium, and modern**, but not luxurious or decorative.
- Error states should use the brighter semantic red rather than the darker burgundy brand color.
